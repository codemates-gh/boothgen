Between Christmas and Valentine's Day, a lot of couples get engaged, and a lot of newly engaged couples start booking vendors. For photo booth operators, January can bring more inquiries than any other month.

The catch: a couple rarely asks just one booth company. They fill in three or four forms in one evening and book whoever makes it easiest. Very often that's simply whoever answers first.

## Speed beats the perfect reply

When an inquiry comes in, the couple is excited and still looking. An hour later they've moved on to florists. The next day, someone else may already have sent them a quote.

You don't need a perfect, custom reply in five minutes. You need a fast, friendly one that moves things forward:

> Hi Maya! Thanks for thinking of us for your wedding on June 14. That date is open. I'll send you a quote with our packages tonight. In the meantime, is the reception at the same venue as the ceremony?

That message does three things: it confirms the date, it promises a quote with a time, and it asks an easy question that gets them replying.

## Get every inquiry in one place

Inquiries arrive from your website, Instagram, The Knot, email and text. If they're scattered, some will slip, and you won't notice until the couple books someone else.

Put a booking form on your website and send people to it from everywhere else. In Genius, inquiries from your form land in one leads list with the event date and details, so you can see at a glance who's waiting for an answer.

## Make the quote easy to accept

The quote is where many bookings stall. Make it:

- **Fast.** Send it the same day. With packages set up in Genius, a quote takes a couple of minutes.
- **Clear.** Three packages, with the one you recommend highlighted. No pricing puzzles.
- **Easy to act on.** One button to accept online. In Genius, accepting a quote creates the contract right away, so the next step doesn't wait on you.

## Follow up without being pushy

Couples get busy. Many who go quiet are still interested; they just haven't decided yet. A simple follow-up plan:

- **Day 2:** "Just checking you received the quote. Happy to answer any questions."
- **Day 5:** Something useful: a photo of a layout you designed for a similar wedding, or a short note on what guests love most.
- **Day 10:** "Your date is still open, but June books up quickly. Let me know if you'd like me to hold it."

Then stop. Three friendly touches are helpful; ten are spam. Genius can send these follow-ups for you on a schedule after the quote goes out, so none of them depend on you remembering.

## Track what's working

At the end of the season, look at your numbers:

- How many inquiries did you get, and from where?
- How many got a quote?
- How many booked?

If lots of people ask but few book, look at your quote and your follow-up. If few people ask, look at your marketing. Either way, you'll know where to spend your effort next year.

## The short version

- Reply within the hour, even with a short message.
- Keep all inquiries in one list so none slip.
- Send a clear quote the same day.
- Follow up three times, then let it go.

The operators who book the most weddings aren't always the cheapest or the flashiest. They're the ones who make booking easy, starting with the first reply.

Booth Genius started as one thing: a CRM built by a photo booth operator, for photo booth operators. Leads, quotes, contracts, invoices, the client portal, galleries, all in one place.

That's still here. But it isn't the only thing anymore, so the company has a new name: **BoothGen**.

## Why the change

We now make two products:

- **Genie, the photo booth app.** It runs the booth on an iPad: sessions, prints, props, boomerangs, and texting or emailing guests their photos.
- **Genius, the photo booth CRM.** Everything you already know as Booth Genius: the business side, from first inquiry to final gallery.

**Genie runs the booth. Genius runs the business.** One name for the company that makes both just made sense.

## What BoothGen means

BoothGen stands for **booth generation**: the next generation of photo booth tools. It's also the address you've been using all along, [boothgen.com](https://boothgen.com).

## What changes for you

Very little.

- **Your login, your data and your settings** are exactly where they were.
- **Client portal links, gallery links and inquiry forms** keep working. Nothing to re-send, nothing to re-embed.
- **Your plan and your rate** don't change. If you locked in an early-adopter rate on Pro, it stays locked.
- **The CRM is now called Genius** (Genius Pro for the plan). Same features, same place in the app.
- **A new logo and look** across the site and the app.

## What's next

Genie is the big one. If you run standard events (weddings, birthdays, corporate parties, school dances) and want a booth app that's quick to set up and doesn't need a monthly subscription, read [Introducing Genie](/blog/introducing-genie-photo-booth-app).

Genie runs on iPad today, and Android is coming.

Questions about the change? Email [support@boothgen.com](mailto:support@boothgen.com). It's still the same team, and it's still built by an operator.

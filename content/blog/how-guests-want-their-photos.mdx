The print is the souvenir, but the digital copy is the one that gets posted. Guests want their photos on their phone before they're back at their table, and how they get them affects both the guest experience and how fast your line moves.

Here's how the three common options compare.

## Text message

**What guests like:** It's instant and it's where they already are. One tap and the photo is on their phone, ready to share.

**What to watch:** Typing a phone number takes a few seconds per guest, and some guests hesitate to give theirs out at a corporate event.

**Best for:** Weddings, birthdays, parties: anywhere guests want to share right away.

## Email

**What guests like:** It's familiar, and the photo is easy to find later. Some guests prefer it to giving out their phone number.

**What to watch:** It's slower to type on a booth screen, and emails can land in spam or promotions folders.

**Best for:** Corporate events and conferences, where guests are used to sharing a work email.

## QR code

**What guests like:** No typing at all. They scan the code on the booth screen and the photo opens on their phone.

**What to watch:** It only works while the guest is standing at the booth. Anyone who walks away before scanning misses out.

**Best for:** Busy events with long lines, and events where you don't need guest contact details.

## Our recommendation: offer more than one

There's no single right answer, because guests differ even at the same event. Offering text and QR code, and email where it fits, lets each guest pick what's fastest for them.

A few tips for setting it up:

- **Keep the sharing screen simple.** Big buttons, a short label and nothing else.
- **Write a short, friendly message** to go with the photos, with your business name. Every shared photo is a small ad for you.
- **Use a photo page, not just an attachment.** A page with the guest's photos, boomerang and your branding looks better and is easier to save than a file in a message.
- **Turn on a live event gallery** for weddings and parties. Guests love scrolling through everyone's photos during the event, and hosts love having them all in one place afterwards.

## Keep the line moving

Sharing is often the slowest step of a session. To speed it up:

- Let guests share from the review screen and start the next session as soon as they're done.
- On busy nights, encourage the QR code. It's the fastest.
- If a guest wants their photo sent to several friends, they can share it from their own phone afterwards. The booth doesn't need to do it.

## The short version

- Text for parties, email for corporate, QR code for long lines.
- Offer more than one, and keep the sharing screen simple.
- Use branded photo pages and a live gallery to make every shared photo work for you.

Get sharing right and your photos travel far beyond the event, with your name on every one.

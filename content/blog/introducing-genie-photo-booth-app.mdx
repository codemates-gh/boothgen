Most of the events on your calendar don't need a laser show. They need a booth that starts on time, prints clean, gets photos onto guests' phones, and doesn't eat your evening the night before.

That's what **Genie** is for. It's BoothGen's iPad photo booth app, built for the standard events you run every week: weddings, birthdays, corporate parties, school dances, fundraisers.

It isn't trying to be the app with every bell and whistle. It's trying to be the one that's fastest to set up, cheapest to run, and hardest to break at a venue.

## Set up once, then every event is minutes

- **Event defaults.** Set your countdown, photos per session, layouts, sharing and printing once. Every new event starts from them, so most events are ready in a few minutes.
- **Prepare from your laptop.** Build the event at boothgen.com the night before. At the venue, open it on the iPad and start.
- **Layouts ready to go.** Fifteen ready-made 4×6 and 2×6 layouts, or design your own in the browser with your client's colors and logo.
- **Pair an iPad in seconds.** Create a pairing code, type it into Genie, done. An attendant PIN keeps guests on the booth screen.

## What guests get

- **Photos on their phone right away** by text, email or QR code, on a page with your branding.
- **Prints** through the BoothGen print box, a small Windows PC connected to your photo printer.
- **Boomerangs, props and backdrops** for the events that want a little extra.
- **An event gallery** you can send your client afterwards.

## Built for real venues

- **Keeps going on bad Wi-Fi.** The booth runs offline. Photos and messages go out when the connection comes back.
- **No texting accounts to register.** Guest texts and emails go out through BoothGen and are included in the event price. No Twilio sign-up, no carrier registration to wait on. Prefer your own Twilio number or email domain? You can use those instead.
- **Two iPads per event.** Run two booths at the same event, or a main booth and a backup.

## Pricing: pay only for the events you run

Genie has **no monthly fee**. You pay **$15 per event**, and that covers:

- Up to 2 iPads at that event (need more? each extra payment adds 2)
- Guest texts and emails
- The event gallery

You can set up and test for free. Until an event is paid, the booth works normally but the screen and photos carry a BoothGen watermark, so pay once the event is booked. Payments are refundable until the event starts and before any photos are taken.

Slow month? You pay nothing.

## Genie and Genius

If you also want the business side (leads, quotes, contracts, invoices, the client portal and automated emails), that's **Genius, the photo booth CRM**. Genie isn't included in Genius Pro, but members pay less for it: **$10 per event instead of $15**. You can start with Genie alone and add Genius later without losing anything.

Genie runs the booth. Genius runs the business.

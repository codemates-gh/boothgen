Most photo booth clients experience your business through a patchwork of emails, PDF attachments, and payment links scattered across their inbox. They're not sure which email had the contract. They can't remember if they paid the balance. When they want their photos, they're digging through Google Drive links from months ago.

This isn't a great experience. And in a world where a competitor is a Google search away, the client experience between booking and event day matters more than most operators realize.

## What a Client Portal Changes

A client portal is a single, branded URL that the client bookmarks once and returns to whenever they need anything. No account creation required — just a secure link that opens their event.

Inside that link, they can:

- **View and download their signed contract** — no more "can you resend the contract?" emails
- **See their invoice status** — what's been paid, what's outstanding, when the next payment is due
- **Pay their deposit or balance** — directly, via Stripe, without you sending a separate payment request
- **Access their photo gallery** — after the event, in the same place they've been going for everything else
- **Send you a message** — a simple thread, attached to their event, that you see in your dashboard

It sounds simple. The impact on client satisfaction is not.

## The Professionalism Signal

Clients make quick judgments about the businesses they hire. A smooth, branded portal — with your logo, your colors, your event details — signals professionalism before you've even shown up on event day. It says: this company has their act together.

Contrast that with the operator who sends a PDF contract via Dropbox, then a separate PayPal link for the deposit, then a Google Drive folder for photos. Clients don't complain about this directly. They just mention it in reviews, or don't leave one at all.

## Payments Without the Chase

One of the most concrete benefits of a client portal is payment collection. When a client can see their invoice, see the balance due, and pay with a card in under 60 seconds — you spend less time chasing deposits.

Booth Genius includes automated payment reminders that fire on the due date. The client clicks the reminder, it takes them to the portal, and they pay. The whole loop closes without a follow-up call or an awkward text.

## Gallery Delivery as an Experience

The gallery moment — when a client first sees their event photos — is one of the highest-emotion touchpoints in the whole booking. Most operators underdeliver it by sending a generic Drive link in a plain-text email.

In Booth Genius, gallery delivery is a triggered automation: you upload the photos, mark the gallery published, and an email goes out automatically with a direct link to the portal gallery. It's password-protected by default, looks polished, and lives in the same place the client has been managing their booking all along.

## The Guest-Safe Share Link

One nuance worth mentioning: clients often want to share their gallery with family and guests, but the main portal link shows invoice and contract information that guests shouldn't see.

Booth Genius solves this with a separate guest-safe gallery link — same photos, no billing details. Clients can share it freely. You maintain client confidentiality.

## Setting It Up

There's nothing to configure. Every event in Booth Genius has a portal automatically. The link is generated when the event is created. You send it with the first communication — quote, contract, or invoice — and it's live from that moment forward.

That one link handles the entire post-booking client relationship.

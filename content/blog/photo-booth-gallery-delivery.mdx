For most photo booth clients, the gallery is the last touchpoint — and in many ways, the most memorable one. It's the moment they relive the event, share it with family, and form their final impression of your business.

Most operators deliver photos via Google Drive or Dropbox. It works. But it's not an experience. It's a file transfer.

## The Problem With Generic File Sharing

Google Drive and Dropbox are built for file storage, not client experience. There's no branding, no context, no connection to the rest of the booking. The client gets a link, clicks it, and is in Google's UI — which has nothing to do with your business.

There's also a practical problem: if your Drive link is the same one you shared months earlier with a contract or venue map, the client might accidentally click into something you didn't intend them to see.

And then there's the long-term reliability issue. Drive links expire. Folder permissions change. A client trying to re-download photos a year later often hits a broken link — and it's your name attached to that broken experience.

## What a Purpose-Built Gallery Looks Like

Booth Genius includes a gallery delivery system (Gallery Genius) designed specifically for post-event photo delivery. Here's how it works:

**Upload and organize** — after the event, you upload photos directly to the event's gallery. Photos are stored on Cloudflare R2 — fast, reliable, global CDN.

**Set access control** — galleries can be password-protected (you set the password and share it with the client) or public. You decide per event.

**Publish and auto-deliver** — when you mark the gallery as published, an automated email goes out to the client: "Your photos are ready!" with a direct link to the gallery in their portal.

**Guest-safe sharing** — the client's portal link includes invoice and contract information that guests shouldn't see. Booth Genius generates a separate guest share link — same photos, no billing details.

## The Gallery as a Marketing Asset

Your galleries don't just serve your current clients — they're a marketing asset. A client who receives a beautiful, easy-to-navigate gallery of their wedding or corporate event is far more likely to share it. Every photo shared is your work in front of new potential clients, many of whom will ask "who did the photo booth?"

A generic Google Drive link doesn't inspire sharing. A polished, branded delivery does.

## Expiry and Deletion Management

Gallery storage isn't free forever, and most clients don't need permanent access. Booth Genius supports configurable gallery expiry — galleries automatically expire after a set period (typically 30–90 days, configurable by you). Before expiry, an automated reminder goes out: "Your gallery expires in 7 days — download your photos now."

This keeps your storage clean and costs predictable, without you having to manually delete galleries one by one.

## What Operators Say

The operators who switch to a purpose-built gallery delivery system consistently report the same thing: fewer client questions after the event. "When will I get my photos?" disappears almost entirely — because clients receive an automatic notification the moment the gallery is ready, and the link they click delivers a polished experience.

That's one fewer thing to manage. Times every event. Every year.
